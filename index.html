    Only the two captains bid, out loud on the call. Everyone else watches the board update live.

    When the hammer falls you get an email with your team, your price and your squad.

  • Captains call it out

    Only the two captains bid, out loud on the call. The organizer enters each bid and everyone follows the live board.

  • Keep enough for a full squad

    A bid is refused if it would leave the captain unable to afford the minimum squad at base prices.

  • The organizer closes each player

    When no captain raises, the highest bidder gets the player.

  • Unsold players come back

    Anyone nobody bids on returns for a second round at the end.
